# Explore

From rest area you get option to explore. At the end of the explore you change to a different room. Examples:

- Waterfall Room (Mind train, water)
- Dark mountain place (Harder Fights/0% Rest/Dangerous training)
- Tall Walls Room (Normal train, normal fights)
- Offering Room (Sacrifice (Consult the Victims), normal fights  )
- Collumns Room (Same as tall walls)
- Boulders Room (Train strength)
- Gate Room (Story Fight)
- Furnace Room (Cooking)
- Campfire Room (Cooking, can be created via campfire item, 50% Rest)
- Ring Room (Enhance TECH)
- Garden Room (Find fruits, 75% Rest)
- Kitchen Room (Cooking + Water)

# Rest

You can idle to heal up to a certain %. Most rooms are at 25% Rest

# Train

You can generally train in most rooms. You can have a 'Waterfall' room which helps with training mind. Or a 'Boulders' room which determine the stat which gets trained. Otherwise you train a stat at random/can fail

# Craft

A 'waterfall' room or a fountain room might provide water to use in crafting recipies

## Craftin Recipie

- Flour + Water = Starter (Unfermented - 24 hours to ferment)
- Starter + Water = x4 Yeast (After another day)
- Yeast + Fruits = Wine (Unfermented)
- Yeast + Wild Roots? + Water = Fermented Medicine
- Yeast + Flour + Water = Bread (Uncooked)
- Sacred Coals + Wood = Campfire
- Honey + Water + Yeast = Mead (Unfermented)
- Stale Bread + Water = Gruel
- Honey + Herbs = Salve (healing item)
